Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Mealie Mobile is an iOS and iPadOS app for people who run their own Mealie recipe server. This policy covers the Mealie Mobile app, its "Save to Mealie" share extension, and this website at mealiemobile.com. The short version: Mealie Mobile collects no data. The app talks only to the Mealie server you sign in to, and we, the developer, never receive anything you do in it.

1. Data We Collect

None. Mealie Mobile has no account with us, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We operate no server that the app connects to, so there is nowhere for your data to reach us.

2. Your Mealie Server

Everything the app shows and changes (recipes, photos, comments, ratings, meal plans, shopping lists, and On Hand foods) lives on your own Mealie server, at the address you enter when you sign in. The app reads and writes that data through Mealie's API, and it is stored and governed by whoever operates that server, which is usually you or your household.

Some Mealie features run on your server and may contact other services, depending on how your server is configured:

  • Importing from a link asks your Mealie server to fetch and scrape that web page.
  • Importing from photos or text sends the photos or text to your Mealie server, which passes them to whichever AI provider its administrator has configured. Mealie Mobile does not choose or contact that provider.

3. Sign-in & Credentials

You can sign in with your Mealie username and password, or with an API token you create in Mealie.

  • If you use a password, it is sent only to your Mealie server, used once to create a long-lived API token named "Mealie Mobile", and then discarded. Your password is never stored.
  • The API token and your server address are stored on your device in the iOS Keychain and in storage shared only with the app's own share extension, so "Save to Mealie" can reach your server.
  • Signing out deletes them from your device and, if the app created the token, revokes it on your server.

4. Reminders

If you turn on Reminders sync, the app asks for access to Reminders so it can keep one list you choose in sync with a Mealie shopping list. It reads and changes only that list. Item names and checked state are copied between Reminders and your Mealie server, and each synced reminder stores a mealiemobile:// link in its URL field so the pair can be matched later.

Your reminders are stored by Apple on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your iCloud account, under Apple's Privacy Policy. A small record of which reminder is paired with which Mealie item is kept on your device. You can turn sync off at any time, and you can revoke access in iOS Settings.

5. Camera & Photos

The app asks for camera access only when you take a photo of a recipe to import. Photos you choose from your library, for importing or as a recipe's picture, use the iOS photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select. In every case the photo is uploaded to your Mealie server and nowhere else.

6. Local Network

If your Mealie server is on your home network, iOS asks permission for the app to connect to devices on your local network. It is used only to reach the server address you entered.

7. Notifications & Background Refresh

  • Cook timers use local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a push service.
  • Background App Refresh lets the app sync your grocery list with Reminders while it's closed, by contacting your Mealie server. You can turn it off in iOS Settings.

8. Siri & Shortcuts

The "Add to groceries in Mealie" and "Sync groceries" actions run inside the app and send the item to your Mealie server. Requests you make through Siri are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

9. Share Extension

When you use "Save to Mealie" from the share sheet, the extension reads only the link you shared and sends it to your Mealie server to import. It does not read anything else from the app you shared from.

10. Data on Your Device

The app keeps a few settings on your device, such as your display preferences, Reminders sync options, and sync state. Diagnostic logs are written to the device's own system log and are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of this.

11. This Website

This website uses no cookies, analytics, or tracking. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and the App Store badge from Apple, which receive the standard information any web request includes, such as your IP address.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone, because we never receive any. The only parties involved in using the app are the operator of your Mealie server and Apple, for Reminders, iCloud, Siri, and the App Store.

13. Data Retention & Deletion

Your recipes, plans, and lists are kept on your Mealie server for as long as its operator keeps them. Deleting the app removes the stored token, settings, and sync records from your device. To delete data on your server, use Mealie itself or ask its administrator. We hold no copy of anything to delete.

14. Children's Privacy

Mealie Mobile does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. It collects no personal information from users of any age.

15. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with a new date. We can't notify you in the app, because we hold no contact information.

16. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@mealiemobile.com.

Mealie Mobile is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Mealie project. Apple Inc. is not responsible for the content of this privacy policy.
